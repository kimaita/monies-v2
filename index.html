Install it and allow SMS access

Monies only reads messages from M-PESA and Airtel Money. It ignores the rest of your inbox.

Find any transaction.

Your M-PESA and Airtel Money history in one place, and you can search it.

  • Look up a person, a business or an amount. People and businesses that match show up above the results.
  • Narrow it down by type, category, date, or money in and out.
  • Tap a transaction to see the amount with its sign, the fee, the code (which you can copy) and the full message.
  • Common businesses such as KPLC, Java House and Safaricom Home are given a category automatically. You can change any category or make your own, and the reports update to match.
  • You can rename a person or business, or merge two that are the same.

Follow your money from any point.

Pick a moment, or a transaction, and see everything that happened after it.

  • Tap Trace since this on any transaction, or choose a day and time under Since in the filters.
  • A summary card shows how many transactions there were, money in and out, fees, and your balance then and now.
  • Each row shows the balance its message gave, so you can check it one payment at a time.
  • Search and the other filters still work while you trace.

Your spending in detail.

Pick a period and Monies works out the figures. There are four tabs: Overview, Activity, Spending and Cash flow.

  • Overview: what you've spent so far against the same point last time, where the month is heading, and the regular payments still due.
  • Spending: categories shown as boxes sized by how much they took, marked when they changed by 10% or more. Tap one for the businesses behind it. Your monthly budgets live here too.
  • Activity: a calendar of the month, shaded by spending, with your streak and the days regular payments are due.
  • Cash flow: your balance with its high and low, why it moved, what you kept each week, and what each type of transaction cost in fees.

Export your data.

Save your transactions as CSV, JSON, Excel or PDF. Large exports run in the background, and you get a notification when they're ready.

  • Pick an account at the top, then a date range: this month, last month, the last 3 months, this year, last year, all time or any dates you choose.
  • To export only some of it, narrow it to money in or out, a type of payment, or one person or business.
  • You can send search results straight to Export, with the same filters.

Permissions, and why

Monies asks for two things when you set it up.

Read and receive SMS Required

So Monies can read your M-PESA and Airtel Money messages. The app can't work without it.

Notifications Optional

Lets Monies show progress while it imports your history or exports a file.

Questions

Can't find what you're after? Ask on GitHub.

How much does Monies cost?

Nothing. It's a free, open-source hobby project. There are no ads and no subscription.

Do I need to enter anything myself?

No. Everything comes from the M-PESA and Airtel Money messages already on your phone. Common businesses such as KPLC and Java House are given a category automatically. You can add or change categories, but you don't have to. See how it works.

Does it work with Airtel Money?

Yes. Monies reads Airtel Money messages too and shows them as their own line, so you can look at Airtel Money on its own or together with M-PESA.

I have two SIM cards. Will that work?

Yes. Each line has its own balance and history. Pick one from the account menu at the top of Home, Analytics or Export, or pick All accounts to see them added together. If one number shows up as two lines, you can merge them in Settings.

A transaction is missing. What do I do?

M-PESA and Airtel change how their messages are worded now and then, so a new format can slip through. Monies lists the messages it couldn't read in Settings, under Unrecognised messages. Copy one, remove your personal details, and post it on GitHub. We'll add it in a later update.

What happens if I reinstall or change phones?

Monies reads the M-PESA and Airtel Money messages on the phone again, so your transactions come back as long as the messages are still there. Your own categories, budgets, renamed names and merges don't come back, because nothing is backed up or uploaded.

Will there be an iPhone version?

No. iPhones don't let apps read text messages, which is how Monies gets its data.

How can I help?

Monies is open source. You can report a bug, suggest a feature or contribute code on GitHub.
