From text message to tidy record.

You keep using M-PESA as normal. Monies does the bookkeeping.

  1. Install it and allow SMS accessMonies only looks at messages from M-PESA. Everything else in your inbox is left alone.
  2. It reads each message for youFrom every M-PESA text, Monies picks out who you paid or who paid you, how much, the fee, the date and your balance afterwards. All of this happens on your phone.
  3. Your history fills inIt works through the M-PESA messages you already have, so your balances and spending are there from day one.
  4. New payments appear by themselvesWhen a new M-PESA message comes in, the transaction is added straight away.

Permissions, and why

Monies asks for three things when you set it up.

Read and receive SMS Required

So Monies can read your M-PESA messages. The app can't work without it.

Phone number and carrier Optional

Helps Monies tell your SIM cards apart if you have more than one number.

Notifications Optional

Lets Monies show progress while it imports your history or exports a file.

Questions

Can't find what you're after? Ask on GitHub.

How much does Monies cost?

Nothing. It's a free, open-source hobby project. There are no ads and no subscription.

Do I need to enter anything myself?

No. Everything comes from the M-PESA messages already on your phone. You can add categories if you like, but you don't have to. See how it works.

Does it work with Airtel Money?

Not yet. Monies works with M-PESA today, and Airtel Money is next on the list.

I have two SIM cards. Will that work?

Monies reads M-PESA messages from both lines, but for now it shows them together. Keeping each number separate is in progress.

A transaction is missing. What do I do?

M-PESA changes how its messages are worded now and then, so a new format can slip through. Tell us on GitHub and paste in the message, with your personal details removed. We'll add it in a later update.

Will there be an iPhone version?

No. iPhones don't let apps read your text messages, and that's how Monies gets its data.

How can I help?

Monies is open source. You can report a bug, suggest a feature or contribute code on GitHub.

What's coming

Monies is a side project, so there are no release dates. This is what's planned.

  • Dual SIM support
